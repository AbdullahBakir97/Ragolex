Travel policy

Business travel is booked through the Fernhill travel desk at least 14 days before departure. Trains are the default for trips under 500 km; flights need approval from your team lead.

The daily allowance for meals on business trips is 45 euros within the country and 60 euros abroad. Hotels are reimbursed up to 140 euros per night.

Travel insurance is provided by the company for every business trip; you do not need to buy your own.
